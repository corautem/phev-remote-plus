## PHEV LINK — quick start
*Remote control for the 2014–2017 Outlander PHEV*

**1 · Get the app**
Scan the QR → download **PHEV‑Link.apk** → open it, allow install when Android asks.
`github.com/Anwo91/phev-remote-plus/releases`

**2 · Car Wi‑Fi**
In the app, enter the **SSID** (`REMOTExxxxxx`) and **password** from the card that came with your keys.

**3 · Pair with the car** (car nearby)
Pick the car from Android's Wi‑Fi list once. After that it joins on its own.

**4 · Register this phone** (only if it stops at "Saying hello to the car")
- Sit in the car, doors closed, power switch **OFF**.
- Press power once to **ACC** (don't touch the brake).
- Within 10 s, press **LOCK** then **UNLOCK** on the key, alternating — **10 presses**, starting with LOCK.
- The car beeps (1 = no phones, 2 = one, 3 = full). In the app: **Settings → Register**.
- Switch power **OFF** to finish. The app reconnects by itself.

**5 · OBD extras** (optional)
Plug in the adapter, then pair it in **Settings → OBD**. Unlocks per‑cell battery, charger and DC‑DC data.

*The car remembers only **2 phones** — a home bridge uses one slot. · No remote unlock (the car's
protocol has none). · Help & source: github.com/Anwo91/phev-remote-plus*

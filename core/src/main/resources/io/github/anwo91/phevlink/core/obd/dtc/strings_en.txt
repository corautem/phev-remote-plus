txt.hv_safety	Safety first: the orange cables and the high-voltage parts (drive battery, inverters, motors, on-board charger, DC/DC converter) carry dangerous voltage, even with the car switched off. Do not open, unplug or measure them yourself; this work is only for technicians trained in high-voltage systems.
txt.srs_safety	Safety first: never probe airbag or seatbelt-pretensioner connectors with a test lamp or multimeter, and never unplug them with the 12 V battery connected. An accidental deployment can cause serious injury.
txt.cause_12v	Weak or ageing 12 V auxiliary battery. On the Outlander PHEV it is charged only by the DC/DC converter (mainly while the car is in READY or charging), and low voltage makes control modules behave oddly or drop off the network
txt.check_12v	Test the 12 V auxiliary battery: after the car has stood for a few hours it should read about 12.6 V or more; with the car in READY the voltage should rise to roughly 13–14.5 V. A battery tester at a workshop or parts shop gives a more reliable answer than voltage alone. Also check that the terminals and ground connections are clean and tight.
txt.check_many_codes	If several unrelated codes appeared at the same time, test the 12 V auxiliary battery before anything else
txt.check_dongle	Unplug the OBD dongle (and any other aftermarket device on the diagnostic socket) when you are not using it: some adapters keep the network awake or drain the 12 V battery while the car is parked
txt.check_dealer_tool	Have a Mitsubishi dealer or a workshop with a Mitsubishi-compatible diagnostic tool (MUT-III or equivalent) read all control units; a generic OBD reader only sees part of the system
txt.check_clear_retest	After a repair, clear the code and drive normally for a few days; if it does not return, the fix worked
txt.check_clear_see	Clear the code and see whether it comes back: a code that returns quickly points to a real, current fault
txt.check_freeze_frame	Note when the code appeared and, if your scan tool shows it, the freeze-frame data (speed, temperatures, load) saved with the code: it shows the conditions when the fault happened
txt.check_oil	Check the engine oil level and condition on the dipstick; top up or change it if it is low, old or sludgy (on a plug-in hybrid the engine runs less, but oil still ages with time)
txt.check_charge_mode	To test the engine for longer, keep it running with the Charge (or Save) function so it reaches full working temperature
txt.check_fresh_fuel	If the engine rarely runs, the petrol may be months old: fill up with fresh petrol from a busy station and let the engine run regularly
txt.read_all	Read all codes; codes stored at the same time often show the cause
txt.hv_cause_12v_relay	Low 12 V supply voltage to the control electronics or relays
txt.pe_coolant_check	With everything cold, check the coolant level in the power electronics reservoir (plug-in hybrids normally have a separate circuit from the engine; see the owner's manual). Never open a hot cooling system
txt.advice_info	Yes. This does not affect how the car drives.
txt.advice_check_soon	Yes, you can keep driving normally. Have it checked within the next few weeks, and sooner if more warning lights come on or the car starts to behave differently.
txt.advice_check_now	Short, gentle trips are usually possible, but get it checked within days. Avoid long trips, towing and hard driving until it is fixed. If the car shows a message telling you to stop, or a warning light flashes, stop as soon as it is safe.
txt.advice_stop	No. Stop as soon as it is safe, switch off and do not drive on. Arrange for the car to be checked or recovered.
txt.advice_misfire	If the check-engine light is flashing, or the engine shakes or loses power, stop as soon as it is safe: unburnt fuel can overheat and destroy the catalytic converter. If the light is steady and the engine runs smoothly, short trips are possible, but get it checked within days. Gentle driving on battery power keeps the engine off most of the time, but the car will still start the engine when it needs to.
txt.advice_hv	The car often still drives, sometimes with reduced power or electric range, but get it checked within days. Follow any message on the dashboard: if it tells you to stop the vehicle, stop safely and do not restart. Do not try to repair high-voltage parts yourself.
txt.advice_network	Usually yes if the car drives normally and no other warnings are shown. If warning lights or messages appear (for example 'Check EV system'), the car may switch functions off; have it checked within days.
txt.advice_unknown	This app cannot tell how serious this code is. If the car drives normally and shows no warning lights or messages, it is usually fine to keep driving while you get the code identified. If a warning message appears (such as 'Check EV system'), the check-engine light flashes, or the car behaves differently, follow the owner's manual and have it checked straight away.
txt.advice_abs	Normal braking still works, but ABS, traction and stability control may be switched off, so wheels can lock under hard braking. Drive carefully, especially on wet, icy or snowy roads, and get it checked within days.
txt.advice_srs	The car drives normally, but the airbags or seatbelt pretensioners may not protect you in a crash. Get it repaired as soon as possible (within days).
txt.advice_throttle	The car may limit engine power (limp mode) to stay safe; a pedal sensor fault can also limit electric driving. Drive carefully, avoid situations where you need full power, and get it checked within days.
txt.advice_steering	If the steering is heavy or behaves oddly, drive slowly and carefully to a workshop or arrange recovery. If it feels normal, get it checked within days.
txt.advice_cooling	Watch the temperature warning closely. If it comes on, or steam appears, stop safely and switch off. Avoid slow traffic, hot weather and towing until it is fixed, and get it checked within days.
txt.advice_oil_low	No. Stop as soon as it is safe and switch off. Check the oil level: if it was low and the warning goes away after topping up, drive gently to a workshop; otherwise arrange recovery. Remember that the car can start the engine automatically while in READY.
txt.advice_isolation	Follow the dashboard warnings. If the car shows a message to stop the vehicle, or will not go into READY, stop safely, do not keep restarting it and arrange recovery. If the car drives normally, get it checked within days and avoid deep water. Never touch damaged orange cables.
txt.advice_dcdc	Limit driving and get it checked within days. If the 12 V battery is not being charged it runs down while you drive, and the car can shut down without much warning. Switch off unnecessary electrical loads.
txt.advice_brake_switch	The brakes themselves normally still work, but brake lights, cruise control or the shift lock may be affected. Check that the brake lights work (ask someone to look) and get it checked within days.
note.turbo	The 2014–2017 Outlander PHEV has a naturally aspirated 2.0-litre petrol engine without turbo- or supercharger, so this code is not expected on this car; have it confirmed with a Mitsubishi-compatible tool.
note.diesel	This concerns diesel engine equipment, which the Outlander PHEV's petrol engine does not have, so this code is not expected on this car; have it confirmed with a Mitsubishi-compatible tool.
note.nox	NOx sensors and NOx after-treatment are mainly used on diesel and lean-burn engines and are not expected on the Outlander PHEV's petrol engine; have the code confirmed with a Mitsubishi-compatible tool.
note.cyl_deact	The Outlander PHEV's four-cylinder engine does not use cylinder deactivation, so this code is not expected on this car; have it confirmed with a Mitsubishi-compatible tool.
note.alternator	The Outlander PHEV has no conventional alternator (the DC/DC converter charges the 12 V battery), so this code is not expected on this car; check the 12 V battery, look for DC/DC converter codes, and have it confirmed with a Mitsubishi-compatible tool.
note.gearbox	The Outlander PHEV has no conventional multi-speed gearbox (it uses a fixed-ratio transaxle with a clutch for direct engine drive at higher speeds), so this code may not apply; have it confirmed with a Mitsubishi-compatible tool.

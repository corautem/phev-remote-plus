# ---- test list
test.dtc-full-scan.title	Fault scan of all control units
test.dtc-full-scan.desc	Reads stored and pending fault codes from the engine, drive battery, charger, motors and other units, and spots typical patterns such as a weak 12 V battery.
test.twelve-volt-health.title	12 V battery
test.twelve-volt-health.desc	Measures the small 12 V battery at rest and with the power on. A weak 12 V battery is the most common cause of odd warnings on this car.
test.dcdc-output.title	12 V charging in READY (DC/DC converter)
test.dcdc-output.desc	Checks that the car charges its 12 V battery from the drive battery while it is in READY, with and without electrical load.
test.hv-capacity.title	Drive battery capacity (health)
test.hv-capacity.desc	Reads the battery computer's capacity estimate and compares it with Mitsubishi's figure for a new battery. Keeps a history so you can follow the trend.
test.hv-cell-balance.title	Drive battery cell balance
test.hv-cell-balance.desc	Compares the highest and lowest of the 80 cell voltages at rest. A large difference points to a weak cell or module.
test.inspection-readiness.title	Ready for EU-kontroll / inspection?
test.inspection-readiness.desc	Checks the engine warning lamp, emission fault codes and whether the car's emission self-tests have finished.
test.charging-session.title	Charging check
test.charging-session.desc	Reads the battery before and after at least 20 minutes of charging to confirm that charge really goes in.
test.engine-warmup.title	Engine warm-up and thermostat
test.engine-warmup.desc	Runs the engine and watches the coolant temperature to find a thermostat that opens too early, overheating or an engine that stops by itself.
test.fuel-trims.title	Fuel mixture (fuel trims)
test.fuel-trims.desc	Watches how much the engine corrects the fuel mixture, which reveals air leaks, weak fuel delivery or a faulty air flow sensor.
test.wheel-speed-match.title	Tyre and wheel speed match
test.wheel-speed-match.desc	While driving steadily, compares front and rear wheel speeds. Different tyre sizes or wear front/rear can trigger EV system warnings.
